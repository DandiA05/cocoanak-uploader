Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

Welcome to Cocoanak Uploader. This Privacy Policy explains how our application handles information when users connect their YouTube accounts and use our video upload functionality.

1. Information We Access

Cocoanak Uploader uses Google OAuth 2.0 and YouTube Data API v3 to provide YouTube upload functionality.

When users authorize the application, we may access the information and permissions required to perform the requested YouTube operations. This includes authorization credentials and permissions to upload videos to the authorized YouTube channel.

2. How We Use Information

Information accessed through Google APIs is used solely to provide the application's functionality, including uploading videos to the YouTube channel selected and authorized by the user.

We do not sell Google user data or use it for advertising purposes.

3. Google API Services

Cocoanak Uploader's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Data Storage and Security

Authentication credentials and access tokens are handled by the application's configured environment to maintain authorized access.

Reasonable security measures should be applied to protect credentials against unauthorized access, disclosure, modification, or misuse. Credentials should never be published in public repositories.

5. Information Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information. Information is shared with Google and YouTube only as necessary to authenticate accounts and perform user-authorized operations.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Users may revoke the application's access at any time through their Google Account security settings. Revoking access prevents the application from continuing to use the revoked permissions.

Locally stored authorization tokens and related application data should be deleted from the environment where they are stored when no longer required.

7. Third-Party Services

The application uses Google and YouTube services. Their use is also subject to Google's applicable privacy policies and terms of service.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
